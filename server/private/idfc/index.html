EDF online autofill help

What is an EDF?

An Export Declaration Form (EDF) records export details for reporting to the authorised dealer bank under applicable RBI and FEMA requirements.

How do I fill EDF online?

Upload an invoice or shipping bill. EDF Ready extracts the details, marks AI suggestions for review, and creates a downloadable PDF.

Which files are supported?

Upload PDF, JPG, PNG, or WEBP commercial invoices and shipping documents up to 10 MB.
